Lasagna

This rich and hearty lasagna features layers of wide pasta smothered in a savory, slow-simmered meat sauce and a creamy blend of Italian cheeses. Baked until bubbling and golden-brown, it is the ultimate comfort food that portions beautifully, whether you are preparing a large family feast or a cozy dinner for two.

The base of this classic dish starts with a rich ragù made by browning ground meat—such as beef or Italian sausage—with garlic and onions. This savory mixture is then simmered with crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, and aromatic herbs until deeply flavorful. For the filling, a rich, velvety blend of ricotta, parmesan, and mozzarella provides a gooey contrast to the robust tomato sauce.
